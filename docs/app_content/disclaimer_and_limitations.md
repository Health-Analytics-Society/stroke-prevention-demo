# Disclaimer and Limitations

## Not Medical Advice

This tool is not a substitute for professional medical care. The risk scores and information shown here are generated by a statistical model trained on survey data and are intended for general awareness only. Do not use any output from this tool to diagnose, treat, or manage a medical condition — always consult a qualified healthcare provider for guidance specific to your situation.

## Educational Purpose

This demo was built as a learning project to explore how public health data can be used to illustrate stroke risk factors. It is meant to inform and educate, not to replace clinical judgment or individually tailored medical advice.

## Limitations

- **Dataset bias:** The underlying data comes from NHANES survey self-reports and measured samples. It may not represent all populations equally, and certain groups could be over- or under-represented in ways that affect model fairness.
- **Simple model:** The baseline model is a standard logistic regression trained on a limited set of features. It has not been optimized, externally validated, or compared against clinical risk tools. Its predictions should be treated as illustrative, not authoritative.
- **Not clinically validated:** This tool has not undergone clinical review, regulatory evaluation, or formal validation against real patient outcomes. Its outputs carry no clinical weight and should never influence medical decisions.
