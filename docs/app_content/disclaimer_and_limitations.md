The result generated is not a medical advice nor is it a diagnosis. Please consult your doctor when you are feeling uncomfortable.

### Limitations

- The data is cross-sectional (a snapshot in time)
- The model is a simple baseline and not clinically validated
- Predictions are estimates and may not generalize to everyone
